CareOPD Privacy Policy

Version 2026-08-30 · Effective 30 August 2026

This Policy explains how personal data is handled when clinics, practitioners and staff use CareOPD. Clinics should provide their own patient privacy notice because they decide the purposes of patient care and record processing.

1. Roles

For account, security, subscription and support data, CareOPD determines the processing needed to operate the service. For patient and clinical data entered by a clinic, the clinic ordinarily acts as the Data Fiduciary and CareOPD processes data to provide the service on the clinic’s instructions. Actual legal roles depend on the circumstances and applicable law.

2. Data collected

  • Organization data: clinic name, contact details, registration details and subscription status.
  • User data: name, role, email, mobile number, professional profile and authentication records.
  • Patient data submitted by clinics: identity and contact information, appointment records, clinical notes, vitals, prescriptions, reports and billing information.
  • Technical and security data: device or browser information, timestamps, audit events, session identifiers and service diagnostics.
  • Communications: support requests and delivery records for verification codes, reminders and account messages.

3. Purposes

Data is used to create and secure accounts; provide appointment, clinical, billing and communication features; maintain audit trails; prevent fraud and abuse; troubleshoot and improve reliability; provide support; administer subscriptions; and meet legal obligations.

4. Legal basis and clinic instructions

Processing is performed for service delivery, legitimate security and operational needs, compliance with law, and consent where applicable. Clinics are responsible for establishing a lawful basis and giving required notices for patient data they place in CareOPD.

5. Sharing

Data may be shared with hosting, database, email, SMS or WhatsApp delivery providers only as needed to operate configured features; with authorized clinic users according to permissions; during a lawful corporate transaction; or when required by law. CareOPD does not sell patient records or use them for third-party advertising.

6. Retention

Account and clinic data is retained while the service is active and for a reasonable period afterward for export, security, dispute and legal requirements. Verification codes expire after ten minutes. Retention of clinical records is controlled by the clinic subject to applicable medical-record obligations. Data that must be retained by law may be isolated and restricted.

7. Security

CareOPD uses access controls, tenant separation, authentication, audit records, encrypted network transport and operational safeguards designed to protect data. No system is completely secure; clinics and users must protect devices, credentials and exports.

8. Rights and choices

Individuals may request access, correction, deletion or grievance handling as available under applicable law. Patients should normally contact their clinic first because the clinic controls their care record. Account users can update certain profile information in the service.

9. Cross-border processing

Service providers may process data in locations where CareOPD or its providers operate, subject to contractual safeguards and restrictions imposed by applicable Indian law.

10. Children

CareOPD is offered to healthcare organizations, not directly to children. Clinics are responsible for notices, consent or other authority required when processing a child’s patient record.

11. Policy updates

Material changes will be published with a new version and effective date. Where required, organization administrators will be asked to accept the updated version.

12. Contact and grievance requests

Privacy questions and grievance requests may be sent to privacy@careopd.com. Include the clinic name and enough information to identify the relevant account, but do not send medical details by ordinary email.
